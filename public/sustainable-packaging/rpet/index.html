What buyers ask before specifying

Practical answers to support a better first conversation.

Is recycled PET the same as APET?

No. APET describes an amorphous form of PET; rPET describes recycled origin. A material can have both attributes, but the terms answer different questions.

Can any rPET bottle contact food?

No. Food-contact suitability depends on the exact recycling process, material, intended use and destination-market rules. Request the relevant evidence for the specific pack.
