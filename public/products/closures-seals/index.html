Buyer questions

What buyers ask before specifying

Practical answers to support a better first conversation.

Is a child-resistant cap available?

Discuss the specific cap-and-bottle system and its applicable test evidence; a cap style alone does not establish certification.

Do I need an induction seal?

That depends on tamper evidence, product protection, opening experience and your filling equipment. Test the assembled pack.
